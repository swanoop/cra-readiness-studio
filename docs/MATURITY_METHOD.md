# Product-security maturity assessment: editorial methodology

## Purpose and scope
The **25-practice core assessment** is a tailored engineering process maturity instrument for manufacturer product-security activities. It is deliberately separate from CRA requirement applicability and conformity assessment. Scores must never be described as CRA compliance percentages.

## Rating guidance
- **1 — Initial:** the specific practice lacks an established, evidenced process.
- **2 — Repeatable in places:** individuals or selected teams conduct the practice inconsistently.
- **3 — Defined:** roles, scope, methods and minimum evidence are approved.
- **4 — Verified:** sampled products and releases show consistent, independently checked application.
- **5 — Improving:** feedback and outcome measures are used to improve the demonstrated practice.

Each of the 25 practices has an original question, five individually authored anchors and suggested evidence. These anchors are editorial guidance, not quotations from legislation or harmonised standards. An assessor must record a rationale and evidence reference for each level.

## Relationship to ENISA
ENISA's SME Cyber Resilience Maturity Assessment Model is an external reference that may inform future comparison. The workbench's core practice list is **not** a verified one-to-one mapping, endorsement, or reproduction of ENISA's model. Do not present its scores as an ENISA assessment.

## Legacy extended catalogue
The 150-control model is retained for compatibility with historical records but contains unvalidated generic anchors. It is labelled experimental and must not be used for external assurance claims before independent editorial and methodological review.

## Assessment quality
Treat a rating as provisional when records are missing, out of date or unreviewed. An average is only descriptive of rated practices and must not be used to conclude that essential CRA obligations are fulfilled.
